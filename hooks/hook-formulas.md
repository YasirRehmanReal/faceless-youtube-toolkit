# 21 Hook Formulas for Faceless YouTube Videos

The hook is the first 5–30 seconds. Its only job: open a loop the viewer wants closed. Every formula below follows the same law — **promise only what the video pays off.**

## Mystery & history
1. **The impossible fact** — "In [year], [person/place] did something that still has no explanation." → *A ship was found sailing with no crew — and the table was set for breakfast.*
2. **The unanswered question** — "Why did [X] disappear / stop / refuse — on the exact day [Y] happened?"
3. **Start at the climax** — Open on the most dramatic moment, then: "To understand how it got here, we go back to…"
4. **The found record** — "A [diary / letter / recording] found [time] later changed everything we believed about…"
5. **The warning** — "Historians still tell visitors never to [do X] at [place]. There is a reason."

## Finance & business
6. **The costly mistake** — "He lost [specific amount] because of one decision most people make every day."
7. **The hidden rule** — "Banks / rich investors / successful shops follow one rule they never advertise…"
8. **Before–after in one line** — "In [year] he was [broke state]. Five years later, [specific result]. The change was not money — it was this."
9. **The counter-intuitive claim** — "Saving more money is making you poorer — if you do it this way."

## Stories & moral / family (Shorts-friendly)
10. **The emotional question** — "A mother hid one truth from her son for 20 years. When he found out, he could not stop crying."
11. **The reversal setup** — "The poor man helped a stranger when no one else would. He did not know who the stranger was."
12. **The last-moment opening** — Start on the final scene's emotion, withhold the cause: "On his mother's last day, he finally understood her silence."
13. **The two-choice dilemma** — "He had to choose: his family's honour, or the truth. What he chose surprised the whole village."

## True crime & documentary
14. **The detail that doesn't fit** — "Every fact in this case makes sense — except one small detail the police missed for 9 years."
15. **The timeline hook** — "At 8:00 he was seen on camera. At 8:07, he was gone — from a locked building."
16. **The official version vs. the evidence** — "The report said accident. The evidence said something else."

## Universal (any niche)
17. **The list promise** — "There are 3 reasons [X happens]. The third one is the one nobody talks about." *(Only use if all 3 are real and the third is genuinely strongest.)*
18. **The myth break** — "Everything you have heard about [topic] is wrong in one important way."
19. **The direct challenge** — "Watch the first 30 seconds. If this does not surprise you, click away." *(Use rarely — and the next 25 seconds must deliver.)*
20. **The specific number** — "It took 11 years, 4 failures and exactly 1 decision." Specifics feel true because they usually are — keep them accurate.
21. **The quiet confession** — (soft voice, no hype) "This is the story [person] never wanted told." Works because everything else on YouTube is shouting.

## Hook self-test (all must be YES)
- [ ] Can the loop be stated in one sentence?
- [ ] Does the video close this exact loop — fully?
- [ ] Is every number/name in the hook accurate?
- [ ] Would the hook still work without music or effects — on words alone?
