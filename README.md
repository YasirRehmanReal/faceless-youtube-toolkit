# Faceless YouTube Toolkit

Copy-paste systems for faceless YouTube channels: hook formulas, title formulas, and production checklists that turn "post and hope" into a repeatable process.

By **Yasir Rehman** — Faceless YouTube Automation Specialist. My full workflow lives in [faceless-youtube-automation](https://github.com/YasirRehmanReal/faceless-youtube-automation); this repo is the quick-drawer version: the formulas and sheets you reach for while a video is being made.

## What's inside

### `hooks/`
- `hook-formulas.md` — 21 proven hook formulas for the first 30 seconds, each with a fill-in-the-blank pattern and an example for history, mystery, finance and story niches

### `templates/`
- `title-formulas.md` — 15 title formulas that open a curiosity gap without lying, with before/after rewrites
- `shorts-script-template.md` — a 45–60 second Shorts script sheet: 3-second hook, one story beat per line, payoff, comment question
- `video-idea-scorecard.md` — score a topic before you produce it: demand signal, hook strength, payoff strength, production cost

### `checklists/`
- `thumbnail-quick-check.md` — the 60-second thumbnail test (mobile size, squint, contrast, honesty)
- `first-30-seconds-check.md` — a ruthless checklist for the part of the video that decides everything

## Who this is for
- Faceless channel owners who publish consistently but package randomly
- Editors and scriptwriters who want a shared standard with their clients
- New channels that want a professional process from video #1

## Honest note
Formulas organise creativity; they do not replace it, and they never guarantee views. Every example here is original, and every rule in these files assumes the video actually delivers what the packaging promises.
