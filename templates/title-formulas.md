# 15 Title Formulas That Earn the Click Honestly

Rule zero: a title is a promise. If the video does not keep it, retention collapses and the algorithm learns to distrust you.

## The formulas
1. **The [Object] That [Impossible Verb]** — *The Ship That Sailed Itself*
2. **Why [Person/Place] [Did the Strange Thing]** — *Why an Entire Village Left Overnight*
3. **He [Did X] — And [Unexpected Consequence]** — *He Helped a Stranger — And Lost Everything (Then…)*
4. **The [Number] [Unit] That Changed [Everything]** — *The 7 Minutes That Changed the Case*
5. **[Specific Amount] Mistake** — *The $10,000 Mistake Almost Everyone Makes*
6. **Nobody Knows Why [X]** — *Nobody Knows Why the Lights Go Out at 3 AM*
7. **[Person]'s Last [Thing]** — *The Captain's Last Log Entry*
8. **What Happened to [X]?** — simple, searchable, honest
9. **The Truth About [Topic Everyone Gets Wrong]**
10. **[X] Years Alone / Silent / Hidden** — *7 Days Alone in the Wild* (duration = stakes)
11. **He Refused [Normal Thing] — Here's Why**
12. **The [Adjective] Secret of [Place/Person]** — *The Silent Secret of the Old Library*
13. **Don't [Do X] Until You See This** — only when the video genuinely prevents a mistake
14. **[Question the audience is already asking]** — lift the wording from your own comments section
15. **Part of a series: [Series Name]: [Episode Hook]** — trains subscribers to return

## Before → after
| Weak | Stronger | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Mary Celeste Mystery Explained | The Ship That Sailed Itself | curiosity gap instead of a summary |
| Tips to Save Money | The $10K Mistake | specific stakes beat generic advice |
| A Mother's Story | Maa Ne Sab Kurban Kiya, Beta Ne Kya Kiya? | emotion + open loop in the audience's language |
| History of the Old Fort | Nobody Enters This Fort After Sunset | a rule with an unexplained reason |

## Title checklist
- [ ] Under ~50 characters where possible (mobile truncation)
- [ ] Main keyword present, but curiosity comes first
- [ ] No word duplicated in the thumbnail text
- [ ] Read aloud: sounds like a person, not a search engine
- [ ] The video's payoff matches this exact promise
