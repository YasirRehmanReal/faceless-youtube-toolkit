# Video Idea Scorecard — Score Before You Produce

Production time is your scarcest resource. Score every idea out of 50 before a single scene is made; produce the top scorers first.

## Decision guide (a guide, not a law)
- **40–50** — produce this week, best slot
- **30–39** — produce, but strengthen the weakest factor first (usually the hook)
- **20–29** — park it in the idea bank; revisit after a related video performs
- **Under 20** — drop it. A weak topic packaged well still loses to a strong topic packaged simply.

## The two veto questions (either NO kills the idea at any score)
1. Can this video be made **honestly** — real facts, no invented claims?
2. Does it respect YouTube policy (no reused content without original value, no misleading packaging)?
