# Thumbnail Quick Check — 60 Seconds

Do this with the finished thumbnail on your phone, not on the big monitor where everything looks fine.

- [ ] **10% test** — shrink it small: is the subject obvious and the text readable?
- [ ] **1-second test** — glance, look away: can you say what the video is about?
- [ ] **Text** — 2–4 words, one line if possible, no sentence
- [ ] **Not the title** — thumbnail words ≠ title words; together they make one complete tease
- [ ] **One focal point** — your eye lands in one place, immediately
- [ ] **Contrast** — subject pops from background in bright light / sunlight mode
- [ ] **Feed test** — screenshot it inside a real YouTube feed next to competitors: does it stand out or blend in?
- [ ] **Honesty** — everything shown exists in the video; no borrowed faces/artwork; no fake results
- [ ] **System** — same font family and colour logic as the channel's other thumbnails

Any box unticked = fix before upload. The thumbnail is half the video's fate; 60 seconds here beats 6 hours of editing regret.
