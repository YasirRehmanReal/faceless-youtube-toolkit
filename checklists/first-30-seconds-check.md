# First 30 Seconds Check

More viewers are won and lost here than in the rest of the video combined. Check the exported file, not the timeline.

## Words
- [ ] The hook starts in the FIRST sentence — no greeting, no "in this video we will…", no channel intro
- [ ] The hook opens a loop (question / mystery / promise) that this video actually closes
- [ ] By second 30, the viewer knows: what this is about, why it matters, and what is coming
- [ ] No throat-clearing: no long credits, no "before we start", no sponsor block before the hook pays something off

## Picture
- [ ] First frame is striking (emotion, place, object) — never a logo or black frame
- [ ] At least 3 visual changes in the first 30 seconds
- [ ] Captions on and accurate from word one

## Sound
- [ ] Voice starts immediately and is the loudest element
- [ ] Music supports, never competes; energy matches the hook's emotion
- [ ] No silence gap longer than a breath in the first 10 seconds

## The stranger test
Show the first 30 seconds to someone who knows nothing about the video (or imagine it cold):
- Can they say what the video is about?
- Can they say what question they are waiting to have answered?
- Do they want the next 30 seconds?

Two out of three is a fail. Rewrite the hook — it is always cheaper than re-editing the whole video.
